Manifesto

We're not building another lead-gen tool. We're building the one freelancers actually want.

Cold outreach is broken. Buying lists is broken. And waiting for someone to publicly post "I need a website" is a small pond — and everyone's already fishing in it.

There's a better way: find the businesses that should hire you but don't even know they have a problem yet. The plumber whose site 404s. The dentist with no mobile layout. The restaurant invisible on Google. Be the first one to tell them.

Freelancers deserve tools that make them feel powerful, not tools that nickel-and-dime them.

We built LocalLead because the existing tools are either enterprise platforms priced for VC-backed sales teams, or scraped junk lists with a 2% hit rate. Neither was made for the person doing the work alone.

You shouldn't need a sales ops team to find clients in your own city. You need real signals — broken sites, missing SSL, dead reviews — sorted by who needs you most.

If you've ever spent a Saturday afternoon manually checking websites one by one, this is for you.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does your data come from? +
We pull from official local business data sources and public web crawls — the same kind of APIs that power maps and directory listings. No scraped junk lists, no shady backchannels.
Is this legal? +
Yes. We only use official APIs and publicly available information — business names, addresses, phone numbers, and the state of their public website. Nothing you couldn't look up yourself, just done 1,000x faster.
How accurate is the audit data? +
Every audit point is checked live at search time — SSL, mobile, PageSpeed, schema, reviews. If a site went down yesterday, we'll know today. We re-verify before you pitch.
Can I cancel any time? +
Yes. One click, no phone call, no retention gauntlet. Cancel and you keep access until the end of your billing period.
What if my city isn't supported? +
We cover the US, Canada, UK, and Australia at launch, with new regions added monthly. Don't see yours? Tell us — we prioritize by demand.
Is there a free trial? +
The Starter plan is free forever — 10 searches a month, no card. Pro and Agency come with a free trial so you can run real searches before paying.
Do you offer team plans? +
Yes. The Agency plan includes 3 team seats, white-label reports, and API access. Need more seats? Reach out and we'll sort it.
How is this different from Buddy or Apollo? +
Buddy and Apollo wait for buying signals or sell you generic B2B contact lists. We find businesses that already need help — broken sites, weak SEO — in your specific city, before anyone else does.
Will you raise prices later? +
If you join early, your price is locked for life. We may add higher tiers, but we won't move you off the plan you signed up for. That's a promise.
Can I get a refund? +
If LocalLead isn't pulling its weight in your first 14 days on a paid plan, email us and we'll refund you. No forms, no interrogation.
